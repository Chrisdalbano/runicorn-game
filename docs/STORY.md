# Runicorn Zombie Apocalypse: story

Jacob and Esau find an evil genie who offers three wishes, reserved for the two brothers. Jacob wishes unicorns were real. The creatures fill the world with color, and one forms a magical bond with him.

Esau wishes for a zombie apocalypse. The genie grants it literally: the unicorns turn, and Esau becomes the first victim. Jacob's bonded unicorn, Runi, retains the first wish's magic. It is the last uninfected unicorn.

Isaac once kept the genie's lamp. He knows how its wishes work, but he cannot use the remaining wish himself. He guides Runi by radio through the dead city toward the lamp tower, where the genie holds Jacob. Rainbow loops destroy the curse inside them. Each relay brings Runi closer; the Warden guards the final approach.

Jacob's last wish is precise: **"I wish Esau's wish had never been granted!"** It reverses the second wish and its deaths while leaving the first wish intact. Esau returns. The unicorns remain. Runi gets both a world and a family back.

The game tells this through five introductory panels, short district conversations, radio messages, and a three-line ending. The opening and ending use small animated pixel dioramas. The first wish is colorful; the second drains the city to gray. Esau's death is narrated, without an image of an injured child. The third wish restores the colorful scene.

The Trotting Dead, Resident Equine, and The Last of Herd are original wordplay homages. No franchise character, recording, or artwork is used.
