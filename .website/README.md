Runicorn Zombie Apocalypse is a 8-bit fast-paced rainbow 'light-cycle' (See for example, Tron) combat with roguelike elements, where you play as a colorful unicorn in a world that is ending... by some other not so colorful unicorns. 

Pretty gnarly ones, actually. 

Use your rainbow to trap Undead Unicorns and save the world. 

Build your loadout between five districts. Equip the Megacorn blaster, collect mutations, and turn a narrow escape into a screen-clearing comeback. Your loops, pickups and attacks add notes to an electro-rock soundtrack.

Jacob wished unicorns into existence. Esau wished for a zombie apocalypse. Guided by Isaac, the genie's former keeper, you must reach Jacob before the last wish dies with him.

Steer with WASD or the arrow keys. Press Space to dash. On a phone, use the left joystick and DASH button. The Megacorn blaster fires automatically once equipped. Click or press a key to start the music.
